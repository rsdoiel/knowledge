NAME

kb-concept — manage concepts

SYNOPSIS

kb concept add NAME DESCRIPTION [–identifier-type T –identifier-value V]

kb concept list

DESCRIPTION

A concept is a named idea or term that can be linked to projects and observations (see kb-link(1)). Names are unique.

A concept may also represent a scholarly entity — a paper, person, institution, or funder — by setting –identifier-type (e.g. doi, orcid, ror, fundref) and –identifier-value (the normalized identifier).

SEE ALSO

kb-link(1), kb-project(1)
